FAQ

Asked most often at this step.

Why is the free licence tied to these two brokers?

It is paid for by the IB rebate on that account. Without the rebate there is nothing funding the licence, which is why an account opened outside the link — or with any other broker — cannot be activated.

Can I move an existing account under the IB link?

Usually not — broker IB tagging happens at registration. Message Telegram with your account number first so it can be checked before you open anything new.

How long does activation take?

Activation is manual and depends on when your details are matched against the IB list. Send the message once and wait for the reply rather than resending.

Can I run the free licence on a second account later?

No. Free access is bound to one MT5 account number on the server. Running more accounts needs the 5-account or unlimited licence.

Do I have to deposit before activation?

The account has to be verified and open. Deposit at least 20,000 USC before running the EA, otherwise the grid has no room and the equity kill-switch may trigger early.

Risk disclosure

A free licence does not reduce trading risk. Trading leveraged gold can result in the loss of your deposit. Grid recovery increases exposure while a position is in drawdown, so floating loss is a normal part of how this EA operates and a large adverse move can exceed your equity buffer. Past results do not predict future results. Only trade capital you can afford to lose.

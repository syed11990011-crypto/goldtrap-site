How it works

One repeating cycle.

No forecasting, no signals to interpret. The EA runs the same four movements, then starts again.

01

Bracket the price

While flat, a BuyStop is placed above the market and a SellStop below it, ready for a break either way.

02

Keep the filled side

The moment one triggers, the opposite pending order is cancelled. Both sides never run at once.

03

Recover with a grid

If price turns against the open side, positions are added at adaptive spacing, pulling basket breakeven closer.

04

Exit as one basket

Take profit is recalculated as the grid grows. When hit, every position on that side closes together.

No. It is an automated trading tool, not a return. Market conditions, account size, lot settings, spread, and your own risk control still decide the outcome.

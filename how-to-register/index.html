Why is the free licence tied to these two brokers?

The free licence is funded by the IB rebate, so your account must be under our IB with Vantage or VT Markets. Other brokers can’t be activated for free.

Can I move an existing account under the IB link?

Yes. Ask your broker’s support to change your IB to ours (IB ID above), then send us your account details on Telegram.

How long does activation take?

Activation is manual, so it depends on when we check your details. Send once and wait for our reply.

Can I run the free licence on a second account later?

No. The free licence covers one MT5 account. For more, choose the 5-account or unlimited licence.

Do I have to deposit before activation?

Your account must be verified and open. Deposit at least 30,000 USC before running the EA, so the grid has room and the equity limit doesn’t trigger early.
